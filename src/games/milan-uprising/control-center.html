      Simulated firmware

      Checking simulated versions…

      This updates our virtual Core, five virtual dice and virtual LED ring to the compatibility versions expected by the official app. No official firmware is installed. Use this button instead of the official app’s bootloader update.

      Versions are saved on this PC and reported after reconnecting Milan. Your game save and board positions are kept.
